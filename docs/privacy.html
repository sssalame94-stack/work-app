Work App Privacy Policy

Work App is a private business application used only by Performance Inc. SAL and its authorized users.

Google Drive access: the app uses the Google Drive permission "drive.file" only to create a "Work App Backup" folder and save backup and CSV files inside it. It cannot see or change any other files in your Google Drive.

Data: business data is stored securely in the company's Supabase database. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.
